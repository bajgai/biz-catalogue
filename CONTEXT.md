# Product shopping

The domain concerns finding comparable retailer offers for a requested product
variant through physical stores and online delivery.

## Language

**Product**:
A named retail item that a shopper is looking for.

**Product variant**:
A specific version of a product identified by relevant attributes such as model,
size, and colour. Variant attributes form part of the requested match.

**Retailer**:
A merchant offering products through physical stores, an official online store,
or both.

**Physical store**:
An individual retail location a shopper can visit to buy a product.
_Avoid_: Using an online catalogue listing as evidence of stock at a physical store.

**Offer**:
A retailer's listing of a product variant at a stated price and currency.

**Official retailer source**:
The retailer's own website, used as the source of its product and availability claims.

**Availability claim**:
A statement from an official retailer source that a product or variant is
available in the stated purchase channel or location.

**Stock quantity**:
A source-reported number of units for a product variant in a stated channel or
location. An availability claim without a count does not establish this number.

**Listed price**:
The product price presented by a retailer. Additional charges may affect the
amount a shopper ultimately pays.

**Total purchase cost**:
The amount a shopper pays to obtain the requested variant through the stated
purchase channel, including applicable delivery charges and taxes. This is the
price-comparison target; unknown components make the total incomplete.
